

# About Rui:

I always knew that Information Technology would be my professional path, a conviction that was reinforced as soon as I started my academic journey and began applying theory to real-world problems.

My first contact with programming was through Java, which sparked my interest in system logic and in understanding how complex systems operate behind the scenes. This curiosity naturally evolved into a strong motivation to design robust, scalable systems and to solve complex technical challenges.

My interest in the Big Data domain emerged during my professional experience at my current company, where I had my first direct contact with large-scale data platforms. Over time, I specialized as a Data Engineer, working with technologies such as Apache Spark, Apache Kafka, Hadoop, Hive, and MapReduce, and leading several data-related projects.

I have extensive experience managing the full software development lifecycle, including technically leading multiple releases. I am also highly experienced in problem-solving within production environments, with a strong background in support activities, where reliability, performance, and fast issue resolution are critical.

Since then, I have continuously expanded my knowledge through hands-on experience and continuous learning, with a strong focus on data architectures, distributed processing, and building reliable data pipelines at scale.
